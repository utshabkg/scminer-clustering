\section{Conclusion and Remaining Work}\label{sec:conclusion}
Under one published label-free rule, applied identically to MICA and a principal component and Leiden pipeline on ten datasets at three seeds, the advantage scMINER reports for mutual information clustering survives in direction on the nine small
datasets once both methods are offered the same candidates. A shared cap alone is not enough, since one reading then
reversed the ranking. The size of the lead still depends on the rule's tie-break, threshold and random draw, MICA's strong
default score is no support, and PBMC14K supports no ranking. These findings are about one rule, and we do not extend them
to mutual information clustering under other rules. The faster neighbour graph is a faster graph rather than a better one:
it needs three to four times the memory to build and gave no better clustering.

\subsection*{Plan through November 18}
The main experiments are complete with this submission. One test remains before the full draft on November 18: drawing pools of 150 and 300 cells from Zeisel and from PBMC14K and rerunning the matched rule at three seeds tests whether MICA's stability flattens when the same data are made small.

\textbf{Still deferred:} annotation with a language model, the hub correction, GPU work, the significance rule in the style of
sc-SHC, the runs on 2,000 variable genes and the label-free runs on the Allen subset. We will ask the instructor whether any of
these belongs in the course project before starting one.

\textbf{The full atlas.} Our Assignment 2 pilot of MICA as shipped on 100,000 Allen cells, slower than Table~\ref{tab:graph}'s rebuild and counting memory beyond the graph build, took 7.1 hours and peaked at 140 GB, which scales to about 6 to
8 days and 3.2 TB for all 2.3 million cells, against the 754 GB on our machine. Our shortlist build alone peaked at 80 GB, about
1.8 TB in proportion, so a faster graph does not close that gap and the whole atlas would need a sketch, clustering a sample and
assigning the rest. We will not change the course-scale plan for the atlas without first discussing it with the instructor.
