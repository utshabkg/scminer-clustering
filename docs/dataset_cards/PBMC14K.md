**Basics**

- **Full name:** Peripheral Blood Mononuclear Cells 14K
- **Short name used in our code:** PBMC14K
- **Paper it comes from:** Zheng, G. X. Y., Terry, J. M., Belgrader, P., et al. (2017), Massively parallel digital transcriptional profiling of single cells, Nature Communications 8, 14049
- Link: [https://doi.org/10.1038/ncomms14049](https://doi.org/10.1038/ncomms14049)
- **What was measured:** Single-cell RNA expression (3′-end transcript counts/UMIs) in human peripheral blood mononuclear cells (PBMCs). Specific immune-cell populations were antibody-purified/sorted before droplet-based single-cell RNA sequencing (10x Genomics); these are blood immune cells, not solid tissue.
- **Species:** Human
- **Tissue:** Peripheral blood
- **Which of our three size regimes it covers:** Ordinary

**Size**

- **Cells before our filtering:** 14,000 in the scMINER PBMC14K subset (seven populations of 2,000 each). The earlier benchmark matrix had 20,000 cells from ten sorted populations; scMINER removed three entire populations to create PBMC14K. [https://jyyulab.github.io/scMINER/bookdown/get-started.html](https://jyyulab.github.io/scMINER/bookdown/get-started.html).
- **Cells after our filtering:** 13,605 using the documented scMINER automatic QC procedure (395 removed, 2.82%)
- **Genes before and after:** 17,986 genes before automatic QC and 8,846 retained after filtering (9,140 removed). The earlier 20,000-cell source matrix contained 21,592 genes; scMINER retained 17,986 nonzero genes when constructing PBMC14K.

- **File size on disk, and where it lives:** on the lab workstation's shared disk, in /media/20TB/shared/sides_data/43.scMINER-Clustering/raw/PBMC14K. It is not on GitHub
- **Size on disk**: Public archive files: 10_PBMC14K.RawData.zip (27.7 MB) and 10_PBMC14K.Processed_eset.rds (22.2 MB).

**Labels**

- **How many labels, and at how many levels**: Seven antibody-sorted immune-cell populations at one supplied ground-truth level: CD14+ monocytes (Monocyte); CD19+ B cells (B); CD4+/CD25 T regulatory cells (CD4Treg); CD4+/CD45RA+/CD25− naïve T cells (CD4TN); CD4+/CD45RO+ memory T cells (CD4TCM); CD56+ natural killer cells (NK); and CD8+/CD45RA+ naïve cytotoxic T cells (CD8TN). scMINER supplies both full and shortened label names, not two independently annotated biological hierarchy levels. [https://jyyulab.github.io/scMINER/bookdown/get-started.html](https://jyyulab.github.io/scMINER/bookdown/get-started.html)
- **Where the labels came from: Antibody-based laboratory purification/sorting before sequencing, inherited from the Zheng et al. purified PBMC reference populations. They were not generated by clustering the PBMC14K expression matrix. scMINER retained seven of ten original populations, omitting CD34+, broad CD4+ helper T, and broad CD8+ cytotoxic T because of sorting purity or phenotype overlap.
- **Why that matters: Clustering-derived ground truth may favor similar clustering algorithms. These labels provide a more independent benchmark for ARI/NMI; however, antibody-based sorting has its own errors, and related T-cell subsets can overlap transcriptionally. Ground truth is therefore operational, not infallible.
- **Smallest label group, with its cell count : 2,000 cells per group before QC (all seven groups tie). Smallest group after the 13,605-cell

**Preprocessing we applied**

- **Quality control:** scMINER reference command filterSparseEset(pbmc14k_raw\.eset, filter_mode = "auto", filter_type = "both"). For cells, require 458 ≤ nUMI ≤ 3,694, nFeature ≥ 221, mitochondrial UMI fraction ≤ 0.0408 (4.08%); no additional spike-in-based cells failed. Combined result: 395 cells removed / 13,605 retained. Individual failures: 174 by nUMI, 0 by gene-count, 222 by mitochondrial fraction, 0 by spike-ins; individual failures can overlap. Auto thresholds use a median ±3 MAD strategy.
- **Normalization:** Counts per million (CPM; library scaling factor 1,000,000) followed by log2(CPM + 1)
- **Gene filtering:** Retain genes detected (nonzero) in at least 70 cells
- **Anything unusual about this dataset:** The scMINER developers first repaired gene symbols against GRCh37.82, selected seven populations from ten antibody-purified samples, and discarded genes absent from the selected 14K cells. The term PBMC14K denotes the pre-QC 14,000-cell collection, not the 13,605-cell filtered analysis matrix. Some of the selected T-cell groups are transcriptionally similar despite different sorting markers.

**Notes**

PBMC14K was used in scMINER/MICA clustering benchmarks and in comparisons of immune-cell regulatory-driver activity. The 2025 scMINER paper highlights difficult discrimination of CD4 regulatory and CD4 central-memory T-cell populations. Benchmark labels arise from antibody-sorted sample identity, not clustering, which reduces circularity in external clustering evaluation. Nevertheless, sorting purity, related immune-cell phenotypes, residual batch effects, and post-filtering class imbalance should be considered. The original study notes expression overlap of purified populations, and the scMINER authors excluded three ambiguous or lower-purity populations.
